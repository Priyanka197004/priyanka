Our story

Started in a kitchen, grown by a promise.

Verdora began in 2018 when our founder started hand-mixing botanical oils on a kitchen counter, frustrated by skincare aisles full of synthetic filler. Every formula since has followed the same rule: fewer ingredients, each one earning its place.

Our mission is to make plant-powered skincare that performs like a clinical product and feels like a ritual — accessible, honest, and kind to your skin barrier.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are Verdora products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes — every formula is dermatologist patch-tested, and our Cloudmilk and Velvet lines are fragrance-optional for reactive skin types.

How long until I see results?

Most customers notice improved hydration within a week, and visible tone or texture change by the 14-day mark with consistent use.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship across the US, Canada, the UK, and the EU, with more regions opening quarterly.

What's your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days. Opened products are covered under our patch-test satisfaction guarantee for 14 days.

Is the packaging really recyclable?

Yes — bottles are refillable glass, and outer cartons are FSC-certified and fully curbside recyclable.
